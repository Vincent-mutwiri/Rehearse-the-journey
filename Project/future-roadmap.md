# Future Vision & Roadmap — Rehearse the Journey (Kioo Companion)

**Problem Statement 6 · Cognitive & Psychosocial Accessibility**  
**Team 6 · Senses Hub, Nairobi**

---

## 1. The North Star Vision
Today, **Rehearse the Journey (Kioo)** proves that a single web application powered by the **AI for Accessibility Toolkit (`AbilityModel` + `Librarian`)** and **Pipecat** can reshape its entire interface, sensory load, reading scaffolding, and conversational pacing for different minds.

Our long-term vision is to turn Kioo from a rehearsal sandbox into **East Africa’s everyday cognitive and sensory travel companion**—helping neurodivergent travellers, people with psychosocial disabilities, and anyone experiencing journey anxiety move through Nairobi and beyond with predictability, dignity, and complete personal control.

---

## 2. Near-Term Roadmap (Post-Hackathon / v1.2 — Next 1–3 Months)

### A. Co-Designer Authored Real-World Journeys ("My Journey Builder")
- **From Fictional Fixtures to Personal Journeys:** Let a person or their trusted support circle author their own real-world journey in plain words (e.g., *Kenyatta National Hospital Clinic 24*, *Huduma Centre GPO*, *Kencom to Rongai Matatu Stage*).
- **Verified Community Playbooks:** Partner with local disability organizations (OPDs), hospitals, and Saccos (e.g., Super Metro, 2NK) to publish **verified, non-hallucinated step-by-step reception and boarding guides** with real photos of entrances, signage, and waiting bays.

### B. Multilingual Kenyan Voice & Sheng / Swahili Parity
- **Authentic Kenyan Speech Synthesis & Recognition:** Fine-tune and integrate local Kenyan English, Kiswahili, and Sheng STT/TTS voices so Conductor Otieno, Receptionist Joy, and Officer Wanjiku sound naturally Kenyan and understand code-switching (*"Shukisha stage ya hospitali tafadhali"*).
- **Swahili Bionic Reading & Plain-Language Simplification:** Extend the Toolkit's `simplify-text` and `bionic-reading` adapters with verified Kiswahili plain-language rules.

### C. Portable Toolkit Profile Across Apps (Adapter MCP Ecosystem)
- **Set Preferences Once, Use Everywhere:** Using the AI for Accessibility Toolkit’s **Adapter MCP** and encrypted profile sync, when Zawadi or Kamau configures their `AbilityModel` in Kioo (*Sensory-Calm Sanctuary* or *Structured Focus & Dyslexia*), those exact adaptations automatically follow them to eCitizen, M-Pesa web portals, hospital booking sites, and transit apps via the browser extension.

## 3. Medium-Term Roadmap (v2.0 — 3–6 Months)

### A. Live "In-the-Moment" Pocket Companion (PWA + React Native Offline Mode)
- **Offline-First Pocket Checklists & Audio Cards:** Matatu corridors and hospital basements often have spotty data. Package approved rehearsal checklists, floor-plan maps, and Swahili/Sheng *Decompress* affirmations into an offline-capable mobile PWA / React Native app.
- **"Show or Speak for Me" Communication Cards:** When a person experiences situational mutism or severe sensory overload in a loud queue, one tap displays or speaks a high-contrast card to the receptionist or conductor (*"I am heading to Waiting Bay B. Please point me to the entrance or write the room number down."*).

### B. Multimodal "Explain This Sign / Queue Ticket" (On-Device Vision)
- **Approval-Gated Camera Sign Reader:** In hospital corridors or government offices with confusing signage or paper queue slips, the user can snap a photo and get an instant **Plainer Words + Audio** explanation of what the sign or form means—strictly bounded with human-verification notices (never claiming obstacle detection).

### C. Live Crowd & Sensory Telemetry ("Sensory Weather Map")
- **Community-Sourced Sensory Ratings:** Combine Google Maps busyness data with crowd-sourced neurodivergent comfort ratings for Nairobi spaces (lighting glare, matatu stage noise levels, quiet waiting corners, and calmest visiting hours).

## 4. Long-Term Impact (v3.0 — 6–12+ Months)

1. **Institutional Adoption ("Kioo-Ready" Public Services):**
   Partner with Nairobi county hospitals, Huduma Centres, universities, and transit operators to embed **"Rehearse Your Visit"** links directly on appointment SMS confirmations—so every patient or citizen can preview the building layout and rehearse the check-in before they leave home.
2. **Contributing Back to the Open-Source AI for Accessibility Toolkit:**
   Upstream our domain adapters (`sensory-calm`, React-safe `bionic-reading` Unicode code-point handling, canvas reduced-motion bridges, and `stop-auto-advance` speech-audio exclusions) into the global AI for Accessibility Toolkit catalog so developers worldwide can build cognitive-accessible apps out of the box.
3. **Self-Paced Confidence Without Gamification Pressure:**
   Provide private, opt-in distress-shift reflections (the anxiety-ladder pattern) so individuals can see their own confidence grow over time—with zero streaks, zero scores, and 100% user-approved data retention.
